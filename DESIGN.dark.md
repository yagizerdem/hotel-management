## Brand & Style

The brand captures the secluded grandeur of the southern Anatolian coast, where the fragrant pine slopes of the Taurus Mountains plunge directly into crystal Mediterranean waters. The aesthetic balances high-editorial prestige with Mediterranean warmth, avoiding generic gold-foil luxury tropes in favor of an architectural, grounded presence.

The visual style is characterized by:

- **Architectural Flat Modernism:** Precise structural framing, generous negative space, and disciplined hairline borders rather than volumetric or blur-based effects.
- **Editorial Editorialization:** Bold, high-contrast serif headlines deployed in refined sentence case, balanced against understated, airy humanist grotesque typography for functional UI.
- **Biophilic & Topographical Contrast:** Pale, sunlit limestone backdrops juxtaposed against dark pine alcoves and deliberate strokes of bougainvillea magenta.
- **Crisp Structural Framing:** Zero-radius photographic portals that evoke cut-stone porticos, paired with delicate, tactile micro-radii on interactive touchpoints.

## Colors

The palette directly references the microclimate and natural materials of Kemer in a sophisticated dark mode implementation:

- **Canvas & Surface (Deep Pine Ink):** `#17352E` serves as the primary illuminated ground, providing rich nighttime ambiance and deep luxury suites.
- **Text & Anchored Volumes (Limestone White):** `#EEF1EC` acts as high-contrast structural text, footer foundations, and primary reading surfaces.
- **Primary Accent (Bougainvillea Magenta):** `#B83A6E` is reserved strictly for high-conversion actions, reservation triggers, special offers, and delicate key accents.
- **Secondary Accent (Aegean Blue):** `#1C6E8C` provides clarity for utility links, secondary navigational pathways, aquatic amenities, and coastal highlights.
- **Subtle Panels (Warm Sand):** `#E3D9C6` provides gentle contrast for architectural cards, booking panels, spa menus, and inset forms.
- **Structural Lines & Outlines:** Use quiet separation on dark backgrounds for defined structural grids and interactive states.

## Typography

Typography governs the tension between classic resort literature and seamless digital reservation flows:

- **Sentence Case Discipline:** Headlines must remain in sentence case or title case. Avoid all-caps headlines or uppercase button labels; luxury is expressed through natural cadence and optical proportions, not aggressive capitalization.
- **Editorial Proportions:** `Bodoni Moda` brings high stroke contrast and sculptural elegance to room titles, experiential narratives, and section introductions. It should never be set in extreme heavy weights; 400 to 600 weight preserve its delicate serifs.
- **Interface Clarity:** `Hanken Grotesk` handles all informational density, metadata, rates, calendars, and form controls. Its open apertures ensure legibility under outdoor Mediterranean sun conditions.
- **Lead Paragraphs:** Introductory paragraphs under primary headlines use `body-lg` with extended line height (30px) to establish a relaxed, narrative rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column architectural grid with generous exterior margins to evoke the spaciousness of private beachfront villas:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, and 4rem canvas margins. Editorial pairings alternate between asymmetric column spans (e.g., 7 columns of photography alongside 5 columns of text and amenities).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, and 2rem canvas margins. Side-by-side components condense into unified blocks or 4-column cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, and 1.25rem outer margins. Photo hero blocks span edge-to-edge (negative margin breakout) to create immersive horizontal full-bleed sequences.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Major thematic sections are separated by `space-3xl` (6rem) on desktop and `space-2xl` (4rem) on mobile to create unhurried pacing between experiences, culinary showcases, and suites.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain the authentic Mediterranean architectural identity, this system rejects artificial drop shadows, floating bubbles, and blurred elevation:

- **Planar Stacking:** Depth is achieved exclusively by shifting flat surface tones. A dark base supports warm sand panels, punctuated by full-bleed background shifts for evening lounges, private cabanas, and night booking modes.
- **Hairline Framing:** Component borders use 1px solid lines for quiet separation on dark backgrounds. For focused interactive panels or highlighted stay packages, step up to a 1.5px border in Aegean Blue or Bougainvillea Magenta.
- **Zero-Shadow Philosophy:** Cards, dropdowns, sticky booking bars, and modals rely on crisp 1px borders and distinct tonal contrast rather than blur shadows.
- **Inverted Depth:** Sections employ dark pine ink backgrounds with high-contrast typography and subtle border lines for understated contrast.

## Shapes

The shape system employs deliberate geometric duality:

- **Photographic Portals (0px Radius):** All photography, architectural crops, panoramic suite galleries, and imagery containers must maintain crisp 90-degree corners (`0px`). This honors the stone masonry and sharp lines of modernist villa architecture.
- **Micro-Soft Interactive Elements (4px Radius / roundedness: 1):** Primary buttons, secondary buttons, text input fields, selection chips, and date-picker elements use a subtle `4px` radius (`0.25rem`). This provides ergonomic touch affordance without softening the resort's clean architectural lines.
- **No Pill or Bubble Silhouettes:** Do not use circular or full pill-shaped buttons. All containers maintain strict rectilinear composure.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Action (Bougainvillea):** Solid `#B83A6E` background with `#EEF1EC` text, 4px border radius, 0px border. Hover transitions to `#A23260`. Minimum height of 48px, horizontal padding `space-lg`. Text set in `label-lg`, sentence case.
- **Secondary Action (Deep Pine / Bordered):** 1px solid border, transparent background, text, 4px border radius. Hover fills and inverts text appropriately.
- **Tertiary Link (Aegean):** `#1C6E8C` text, no background or border, followed by a hairline animated underline or a delicate rightward arrow (`→`).

### Cards & Accommodation Units

- **Structure:** Crisp exterior with `0px` border radius on the outer image container. Card base uses tailored surface tones with a 1px solid subtle border.
- **Internal Spacing:** `space-lg` padding surrounding room titles, capacity specifications, and rate summaries.
- **Badge Accents:** Special offers or villa tier badges sit in the top-left or directly above the title using subtle panels with contrasting text.

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers

- **Containers:** 4px border radius, background surface, 1px solid structural border. Height 52px for comfortable touch interaction.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#1C6E8C` border without outer glowing rings.
- **Typography:** Placeholder text in `body-md` at 50% opacity; active text in solid high-contrast tones. Floating labels in `label-sm`.

### Chips & Filter Tags

- **Default State:** Transparent fill, 1px solid subtle border, 4px border radius, padding `space-xs` `space-md`, text in `label-md`.
- **Active State:** Solid fill with contrasting text, or `#1C6E8C` fill for amenity filters.

### Lists & Amenity Grids

- **Style:** Clean dividing hairlines (1px solid) spanning edge-to-edge.
- **List Items:** Vertical padding of `space-md`, icon glyphs rendered in deep pine or Aegean blue, paired with `body-md` typography.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls

- **Geometry:** Checkboxes use a 2px radius; radios are circular. Both feature a 1.5px solid border.
- **Checked State:** Solid fill with an interior white check or inset dot. No drop shadows.
